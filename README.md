# customer-churn-early-warning
Customer Churn Early-Warning Model
Overview
Customer churn is often visible before a customer actually decides to leave.

This project explores a Customer Churn Early-Warning Model designed for enterprise Customer Success and Service Management teams.

Objective
The objective is to identify potential customer risk early enough for Customer Success teams to take proactive action.

Key Risk Indicators
Overall Customer Heatmap
Customer Pulse / Sentiment
Order History & Pipeline
CTN / Service Estate Fluctuation
Circuit / Service Cancellations
Incident & SLA Performance
